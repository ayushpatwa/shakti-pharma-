Our Journey

The Legacy of Shakti Pharma

Traced back to the mid-19th century as a dedicated group of manufacturing physicians, we have preserved herbal knowledge across generations.

Mid 19th Century

Foundations Laid

A group of local Vaidyas and physicians in Uttar Pradesh began formulating customized herbal extracts for chronic ailments.

Early 20th Century

Arpi Chemical Industries

The practice evolved into a structured pharmaceutical concern to supply patent medicines to wider regions of Northern India.

1974

Birth of The Shakti Pharma

The firm was formally renamed as 'The Shakti Pharma' in Kasganj, setting up modernized manufacturing units for herbal syrups.

Today

Digital Herbal Store

Delivering high-quality Ayurvedic patent medicines to clients and practitioners all over India with secure order processing.
